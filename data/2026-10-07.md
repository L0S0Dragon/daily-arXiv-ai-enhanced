The bound applies to arbitrary adaptive protocols with quantum memories and constrains the two-way quantum, entanglement-distribution, private, and secret-key capacities. It vanishes throughout the entanglement-breaking region and approaches the corresponding PLOB bound at infinite energy. At finite energy, it improves the evaluated Gaussian squashed-entanglement and PLOB bounds in relevant parameter regimes, while comparison with hashing rates quantifies the remaining gap to achievability. The result provides a common energy-dependent benchmark for noisy bosonic Gaussian channels and extends finite-energy converse methods beyond the quantum-limited setting.
